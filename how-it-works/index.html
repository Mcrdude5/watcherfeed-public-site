How WatcherFeed Works

1. Connect an authorized account

The account owner completes the platform authorization flow using the permissions approved for the integration.

2. Discover and prepare content

WatcherFeed evaluates potential short-form moments and applies quality and safety gates before content can progress.

3. Route by account

Each publishing account can have separate limits and routing rules, including future streamer-specific TikTok accounts.

4. TikTok draft workflow

The initial TikTok integration uploads creator-approved media to TikTok as a draft/inbox item for creator review.

5. Public posting controls

Direct or autonomous posting is enabled only after applicable platform permissions, review, authorization, and WatcherFeed certification gates are satisfied.
