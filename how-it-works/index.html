How WatcherFeed Works

The Watcher Feed is a desktop content-management workflow that prepares creator-authorized short-form clips and lets authorized account owners complete the TikTok publishing process.

1. Connect and identify the TikTok creator

The creator authorizes their own account using TikTok's official sign-in and consent page. WatcherFeed reads only the profile/scopes granted by the account owner. The current draft-upload workflow is designed for user.info.basic and video.upload; it does not require public Direct Post permission.

2. Identify, evaluate and review media

Candidate livestream moments are discovered and captured for local processing. An independent-evaluator rubric scores hook, context, reaction/commentary, payoff, pacing, visual quality and safety. Clips that fail required gates do not automatically become publishable packages. Source/clip ownership and permission must be confirmed before uploading to TikTok.

3. Produce a reviewable video package

Qualified media is prepared for short-form viewing with a clip file, supporting metadata, and a review manifest. Authorized operators can check the clip and planned destination before any platform transfer.

4. Route to the authorized account

Account-specific rules and daily limits control routing. An account must be explicitly connected, enabled and verified before the publishing dispatcher can act. The system retains publication receipts and checks for duplicate or ambiguous results.

5. Upload as a TikTok draft

In the current connector, the approved clip is uploaded to TikTok using the official draft/inbox upload API. This step does not make a public post. The connected creator opens TikTok, reviews the video and posting details, and makes the final decision to post.

6. Revoke access or request deletion

The connected creator can revoke application access in TikTok settings. For help with account disconnection, stored integration data or content rights, see our Privacy Policy or Contact page.

Frequently asked questions

Can WatcherFeed automatically publish public TikToks today?

No. TikTok Direct Post and unattended public posting remain disabled while the relevant app review, scopes, creator-consent rules and connector certification remain incomplete.

Does TikTok developer approval grant permission to repost other creators' content?

No. Account authentication and API access are separate from copyright or creator permission. Content must be owned or otherwise authorized for use.

Where can I request onboarding or support?

Contact watcher@godschoice.net for desktop onboarding, workflow questions and account support.
