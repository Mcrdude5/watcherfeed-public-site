WatcherFeed is a desktop content-management workflow for discovering, evaluating, preparing, and publishing creator-approved short-form clips to connected social platforms.

Discover & evaluate

WatcherFeed identifies promising short-form moments and evaluates them before they are considered for publishing.

Creator-controlled accounts

Social accounts are connected by their owners through official authorization flows and remain subject to platform permissions.

Draft-first TikTok workflow

The initial TikTok integration uses the official draft upload flow so the creator can review and finish the post in TikTok.

TikTok Direct Post and autonomous public posting are not represented as enabled until the relevant TikTok permissions, account authorization, review, and WatcherFeed certification requirements are satisfied.
