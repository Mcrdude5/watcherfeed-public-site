The actual desktop workflow

A creator-controlled path from discovery to TikTok draft

WatcherFeed is an operating tool, not a public video feed or an automatic reposting service. Connected account owners retain control of account access and the final TikTok post.

  1. Authorize your own account

    The TikTok account owner connects through TikTok's official consent screen. The integration only requests configured scopes; the draft workflow uses basic profile access and video.upload.

  2. Review prepared clips

    WatcherFeed discovers candidate moments, captures authorized media, evaluates quality and safety, and prepares reviewable short-form video packages. Not every candidate is approved.

  3. Approve the destination

    Account-specific routing and daily limits decide which authorized account may receive a package. A clip is not made public merely because its automated quality score passes.

  4. Finish inside TikTok

    The initial supported flow sends approved media to TikTok's draft/inbox flow. The creator reviews the content and completes the public posting action in TikTok.

What WatcherFeed does

Local discovery, captured-media evaluation, clip preparation, account routing, publication receipts and operational review.

Current TikTok integration

Draft upload: Implemented as a permission-gated connector. Direct public posting: Not currently enabled. Approval of the TikTok developer app does not itself grant WatcherFeed permission to publish videos without creator consent.

Creators must own or have permission to use the content. WatcherFeed does not grant rights to reuse another creator's stream or recordings.

TikTok Direct Post and autonomous public posting are not represented as enabled until the relevant TikTok permissions, account authorization, review, and WatcherFeed certification requirements are satisfied.
