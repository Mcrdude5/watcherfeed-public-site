Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This policy explains how WatcherFeed handles information when an authorized user connects or uses supported social-platform integrations.

Information processed

  • Basic connected-account information made available through authorized scopes, such as display name, avatar, platform account identifier, and granted permissions.
  • Access and refresh tokens needed to maintain a connected account.
  • Media, transcripts, clip metadata, scores, titles, descriptions, tags, and processing results needed to evaluate or prepare content.
  • Operational health, queue, receipt, and diagnostic information used to maintain reliability.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, identify the connected creator, process authorized media, prepare or upload drafts, complete requested platform actions, prevent duplicate actions, enforce safety controls, and troubleshoot failures.

TikTok data

WatcherFeed requests only the TikTok scopes configured for the integration. The initial integration is designed around basic profile identity and draft upload. TikTok data is not sold or used for third-party advertising.

Local processing

WatcherFeed primarily operates as a desktop workflow. Sensitive credentials are designed to stay in protected local application storage. Private operational telemetry is designed to exclude raw media, raw transcripts, OAuth tokens, refresh tokens, and client secrets.

Sharing

Information is shared only as necessary to perform requested integrations with platforms such as TikTok or YouTube and with infrastructure providers used for private source control, monitoring, or hosting. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Authorization data is retained only while needed to maintain the connected account or until access is revoked or disconnected. Local media, artifacts, and logs may be retained according to application retention settings.

Revocation and deletion

Users may revoke WatcherFeed's TikTok authorization through TikTok account/app settings. Requests to delete WatcherFeed-held account information or locally retained integration data may be sent to watcher@godschoice.net.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect credentials and operational information.

Contact

For privacy, deletion, or support requests, contact watcher@godschoice.net.
